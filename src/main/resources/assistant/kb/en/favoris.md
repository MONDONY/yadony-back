For a trip, open it and tap the save button in its details. For a request, tap the save button on its card, or the bookmark at the top of its details. Tap again to remove it.
Find them with the saved-items button of the Search tab, in "Saved", which separates a Trips tab and a Requests tab.
Saved items stay accessible even when you are not signed in.
On each trip card in search, the "like" heart is not the save button. A like is public (one per person and per trip, not possible on your own trip), its counter shows from 1 like, and it needs an account (without one, the app invites you to sign in). Saving (the bookmark) stays private and open to visitors. Liked trips rise a little in the search ranking.
Sharing (a curved arrow) opens the trip poster, as before.
As a traveler, you see the number of likes on your trip on its card and on the "Poster" tile of its screen, plus the number of people who viewed your trip and of poster views; only you can see these figures.
