For a trip, tap the bookmark under its card in search. For a request, tap the save button on its card, or the bookmark at the top of its details. Tap again to remove it.
Find them with the saved-items button of the Search tab, in "Saved", which separates a Trips tab and a Requests tab.
Saved items stay accessible even when you are not signed in.
On each trip card in search, the "like" heart is not the save button. A like is public (one per person and per trip, not possible on your own trip), its counter shows from 1 like, and it needs an account: a visitor without one is invited to sign in to like or share, but can still save a trip (the bookmark stays private). Liked trips rise a little in the search ranking.
Sharing (a curved arrow) opens the trip poster, as before.

The like counter is public on cards. As a traveler, you see yours on your own card in search, read-only. On a live trip, the "Poster" tile of the trip screen also gives the number of likes, the number of people who viewed it and the poster views: only these last two figures are visible to you alone.
