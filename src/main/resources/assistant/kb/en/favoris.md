For a trip, tap the bookmark under its card in search. For a request, tap the save button on its card, or the bookmark at the top of its details. Tap again to remove it.
Find them with the saved-items button of the Search tab, in "Saved", which separates a Trips tab and a Requests tab.
Saved items stay accessible even when you are not signed in.
In the latest version of the app, under each trip card in search you find the "like" heart, share and the bookmark: the heart is not the save button (saving is the bookmark, which stays private). One like per person and per trip, including on your own trip, and it needs an account: a visitor without one is invited to sign in to like or share, but can still save a trip (the bookmark stays private). Liked trips rise a little in the search ranking.
Sharing (a curved arrow) opens the trip poster, as before.

The number of likes shows on the card from the first like, whether or not you have liked the trip (at zero, the heart shows alone). As a traveler, you can like your own trip and you see the number of likes like everyone else. On a live trip, the "Poster" tile of the trip screen also gives the number of likes, the number of people who viewed it and the poster views: only the last two figures (trip views, poster views) are visible to you alone.
