Your profile is edited from Profile › ☰ › Edit profile.
There you fill in your first name and last name (optional: without them, other members see your account's automatic nickname, never your phone number or email), bio, city and spoken languages.
Email and phone are added there too, each with a verification code; once saved, they cannot be replaced in the app.
A "Profile complete" gauge shows what is left to fill in.
