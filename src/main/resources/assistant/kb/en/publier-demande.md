To start, tap the Publish button of the Search tab then "Send a parcel" (or "Send a parcel" on the "I'm sending" tile of the Activity tab, while you have no parcel).
After the sender commitments screen, which you must accept, the request is filled in three steps: The trip (departure and arrival cities, then the date and its flexibility if you have one), The parcel (approximate weight, content, description, photos) then The budget.
For the price, choose "I'm open to offers" (travelers propose their price) or "I set my price" (a firm amount), then the accepted payment methods.
A preview lets you check before "Post my request", or save it as a draft.
The date is optional. Without a date, the request shows "Flexible date": every traveler on the route can offer their departure, whatever its date, and the request closes on its own 60 days after it was posted if no deal is made. With a date, it must fall within the next 90 days.
Once posted, travelers on that route are notified and you get a notification at the first offer.
