In Activity, open "My alerts" then tap "Create" to be notified as soon as a traveler or a parcel appears on your route.
Choose parcels or trips, the cities, a date window, a minimum weight and the notification mode: instant, daily or silent.
An alert can be edited, duplicated, paused or extended once it has expired.
Even without an alert: when you search for a trip while signed in and the search finds no traveler, Yadony remembers it for 30 days. If a matching trip (same cities, within your dates) is posted, you are told once, by notification and by email if your account has an address, at most twice a week. To hear about every new trip, create an alert instead.
