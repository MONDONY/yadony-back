title: Payment methods (card, cash, mobile money)

---
The payment methods are bank card, cash and mobile money. Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal are coming soon: they are not offered yet, and the app will show them as soon as they are enabled.
The traveler chooses which ones they accept on their trip, and the sender picks among them when paying; they also depend on the currency (mobile money is offered for trips in XOF or XAF, otherwise only card and cash, or cash only).
By card, the money is held by Yadony until delivery is confirmed; by mobile money, it is also held by Yadony until delivery, and the traveler must have a payout account for the chosen operator.
Online, the payment sheet offers bank card. Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal only appear there once they are enabled and your device supports them.
In cash, you hand the sum to the traveler at handover: there is no escrow.
