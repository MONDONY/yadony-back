At handover, the sender shows the parcel QR (Activity › My parcels › your shipment › Show the drop-off QR) and the traveler scans it in the Tracking tab, then takes a photo of the parcel: the photo is required to validate pickup.
The parcel then moves to "Handed to the traveler".
The traveler checks the parcel at handover: if it doesn't match the declaration, they can refuse it from the parcel details ("Options" › "The parcel doesn't match"), with a photo, and the sender is refunded. If it is worth more than planned, the traveler can request an extra charge ("Options" › "Request an extra charge"): the handover scan is blocked until the sender answers.
If the sender had added kilos or items to an accepted parcel and doesn't bring them, the traveler taps "The extra kilos are not there": the change is cancelled and the original parcel goes ahead at the agreed price. A change waiting for an answer or a payment also blocks the handover scan.
Handover must happen before the trip's drop-off deadline. This date and time are shown in the departure city's local time. Once they have passed, no new request can be made on the trip (it disappears from search and favorites), a request not yet accepted is canceled and any payment is fully refunded.
Be present at the agreed handover place: in case of a no-show, you can report it.
