Open Profile › Money › Get paid, then connect your bank account: the money is then transferred automatically to the account linked to your IBAN after each confirmed delivery.
Your identity must be verified first, otherwise the row stays greyed out.
This row does not appear if activation isn't possible in your country of residence, or if the account is already active.
What counts is the country of residence in your profile (if it is empty, the app shows "Not available yet in your country" with an "Update my country of residence" button, which opens Settings › Preferences), not where you are; without activation, you can still be paid in cash or by mobile money.
If you started without finishing, Yadony reminds you the next day and again a week later: by email if your account has an address, with a "Finish my setup" button that reopens the Stripe form where you left off (valid 14 days), otherwise by notification.
