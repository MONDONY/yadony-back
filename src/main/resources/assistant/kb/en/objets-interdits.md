title: Which items are forbidden?
keywords: forbidden items, prohibited, weapons, drugs, cash, liquids, dangerous products, allowed content, customs, what can I send, perishable
---
Every sender commits to sending only lawful, authorized content.
Forbidden items notably include cash, weapons, drugs, flammable liquids and dangerous products.
The traveler may refuse a parcel, including at customs, and states on their trip what they accept or refuse, for example liquids or perishable goods.
Forbidden content can be reported with the reason "Prohibited item".
