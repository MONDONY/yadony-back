title: Which items are forbidden?
keywords: forbidden items, prohibited, weapons, drugs, cash, liquids, dangerous products, allowed content, customs, what can I send, perishable

Every sender commits to sending only lawful, authorized content.
Forbidden items notably include: drugs and narcotics, weapons, explosives and hazardous materials, flammable liquids, cash, counterfeits or goods prohibited by customs, live animals, official documents (passports, identity cards, residence permits), and any item prohibited by the law of the departure, destination or transit country.
Before sending, the sender electronically signs ("I sign & accept") a warning about prohibited goods and their customs liability; the traveler reviews this declaration and can refuse a non-compliant parcel at handover, including at a customs check.
The traveler also states on their trip what they accept or refuse, for example liquids or perishable goods.
Forbidden content can be reported with the reason "Prohibited item".
