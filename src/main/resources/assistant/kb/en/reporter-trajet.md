If your departure is canceled (flight canceled, breakdown) or postponed, open the trip in My trips then tap "Reschedule".
Choose the new departure date, the reason and, if you like, a message to senders. The first reason follows the trip's mode: "Flight canceled" (air), "Breakdown or setback" (road) or "Transport canceled" (other modes, such as boat when it is offered). The other two are "Trip postponed" and "Other reason".
Senders whose parcel is accepted can keep it or cancel at no cost.
A trip can be rescheduled twice at most, and no longer once a parcel is on its way, arrived or delivered.
Once the trip has left, it is a delay, not a reschedule: the traveler announces the new estimated arrival range, a reason (port congestion, boat only; bad conditions; extended stopover; transport disruption; other) and a message of 300 characters at most. Senders of an undelivered parcel are notified by notification, e-mail and a message in the conversation; nothing is asked of them and handovers do not move. At most 5 delays per trip in 24 hours.
