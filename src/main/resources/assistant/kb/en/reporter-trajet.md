If your flight is canceled or your trip postponed, open the trip in My trips then tap "Reschedule".
Choose the new departure date, the reason (Flight canceled, Trip postponed or Other reason) and, if you like, a message to senders.
Senders whose parcel is accepted can keep it or cancel at no cost, and each cancellation can weigh on how visible your trips are.
A trip can be rescheduled twice at most, and no longer once a parcel is on its way, arrived or delivered.
