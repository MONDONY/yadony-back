When boat is offered and a parcel is accepted by a verified carrier (GP Pro or PRO account) on a boat trip, the carrier is paid when the parcel is dropped off, not on delivery. The rule is set when the parcel is accepted: a parcel accepted otherwise stays paid on delivery.
At drop-off, the carrier scans the parcel's QR code that the sender shows in their app, then takes a photo of the dropped-off parcel. Without the sender's QR code or without the photo, the drop-off is refused.
Once the carrier has been paid at drop-off, nothing is refunded automatically: cancellation by the sender or by the carrier, refusing the parcel and canceling the trip are refused. Contact support, who opens a dispute. The price no longer changes after drop-off: no extra charge. A parcel paid in cash follows the same drop-off rule (QR code and photo required, price fixed) but no money goes through Yadony: it can still be canceled as before, like a parcel whose payout hasn't gone out yet.
On a trip sold by the cubic meter, a request or a price offer requires the parcel's dimensions (length, width, height). A traveler can make an offer on a boat request without giving kilos. Before a negotiated deal is paid, Yadony checks that the volume still fits on the trip: if not, nothing is charged and the sender picks another trip.
Before drop-off, the sender can add volume to their accepted parcel ("Edit my parcel"): in m³, at the trip's price per m³, service fee included, within the free volume. The addition is paid out to the carrier with the parcel, at drop-off.
