The Search tab shows available travelers when you choose "I'm sending a parcel", and parcels to carry when you choose "I'm traveling".
Filter by departure and arrival cities, date, rating, kilos, price, parcel size, urgency or "Near me". Less common settings (contents, transport, rating, stops, payment) are under "More filters".
The full-screen map shows each listing with its price per kilo and the flag of the arrival country. Tap a price to see the trip, then the summary card to open the listing. The "List" button shows the results as a list, and "Map" takes you back to the map. With "Near me" (location button), you pick a radius from 5 to 200 km and only listings inside that radius stay on screen.
If nothing matches, create an alert to be notified as soon as a trip appears.
Departure and arrival cities always stay strict. When fewer than 7 trips match all your criteria, a second block "Other listings on this route" appears: trips on the same route that miss at least one criterion (date, urgency, kilos, transport mode, minimum rating, content type, radius, weekend, GP Pro). Each listing shows why, for example "Departs 12/11 instead of 10/11" or "5 kg available instead of 8". The verified-travelers-only, maximum price per kilo, stops and payment methods filters stay strict.
In "I'm traveling" mode, if you have at least one active trip, the "For my trips" chip is switched on for you: the list shows the parcels that match your trips and its title gives their number. Tap the chip to remove it and it will not come back by itself; tap it again to switch it back on.
