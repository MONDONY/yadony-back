The Search tab shows available travelers when you choose "I'm sending a parcel", and parcels to carry when you choose "I'm traveling".
Filter by departure and arrival cities, date, rating, kilos, price, parcel size, urgency or "Near me". The "Transport" chip, first among the home chips, picks the mode (All, Air, Boat, Road) when several modes are offered. "Road" shows cars and coaches, and adds the "Size" chip (trips that accept at least this parcel size; a trip with no max size set does not appear) and the "Passes through" filter (trips of journeys that stop in the city). For boat, you can also filter by minimum volume, maximum price per m³ and latest arrival. Less common settings (contents, rating, stops, payment) are under "More filters".
The full-screen map shows each listing with its price (per kilo, per m³ for boat) and the flag of the arrival country. Tap a price to see the trip, then the summary card to open the listing. The "List" button shows the results as a list, and "Map" takes you back to the map. With "Near me", you pick a radius from 5 to 200 km and only listings inside that radius stay on screen.
If nothing matches, create an alert to be notified as soon as a trip appears.
Departure and arrival cities always stay strict. When fewer than 7 trips match all your criteria, a second block "Other listings on this route" appears: trips on the same route that miss at least one criterion (date, urgency, kilos, transport mode, minimum rating, content type, radius, weekend, GP Pro). Each listing shows why. The verified-travelers-only, maximum price per kilo, stops, size, "Passes through", volume, price per m³, arrival and payment filters stay strict.
In "I'm traveling" mode, if you have an active trip, the "For my trips" chip is switched on for you: the list shows the parcels that match your trips. Tap the chip to remove it and it will not come back by itself; tap it again to switch it back on.
