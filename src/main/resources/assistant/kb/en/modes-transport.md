Yadony offers transport by air, and by road or boat when Yadony opens those modes. If only one mode is offered, the "Mode" step of the trip form does not appear.
"Road" covers cars, coaches and trucks, within one country or international: up to several tons, no suitcases, an optional max parcel size (S bag, M box, L suitcase or chair, XL furniture) and arrival up to 14 days after departure.
Boat, when it is offered, covers sea crossings (cargo, container) and is sold by the cubic meter: the traveler sets a volume and a price per m³, the sender gives the parcel's dimensions (length, width, height in cm) and Yadony works out its volume. It is reserved for verified travelers (GP Pro or PRO account). The arrival is announced as a date range; if the trip is delayed, the traveler gives the new range and a reason (port congestion, bad conditions, extended stopover, transport disruption, other) and the senders concerned are notified.
The trip's mode is shown everywhere: the mode icon appears on the ticket and on trip cards, and airport codes only appear for air.
The traveler chooses the mode when publishing (for example Marseille-Dakar by boat), and the arrival date cannot be more than 3 days after departure by air, 14 days by road and 120 days by boat.
For a parcel request, "Transport mode" offers Any, Air, and Boat or Road when offered: only travelers of that mode see the request. In search, the "Transport" chip filters trips by mode.
