You are on the search filters ("Filter trips" or "Filter parcels"). Set "WHERE", "WHEN" and "AROUND ME", then, for a trip, "TRANSPORT" (All, Air, Boat, Road: shown when several modes are offered), weight, price and the quick filters (Urgent, 23 kg suitcase, GP Pro, Verified identity). For Road: "PARCEL SIZE" (at least this size) and "PASSES THROUGH" (journeys that stop in the city), with no 23 kg suitcase. "More filters" expands contents, rating, stops and payment, or maximum weight, size and "For my trips" for a parcel. "Clear all" resets everything, "Search" starts the search.
