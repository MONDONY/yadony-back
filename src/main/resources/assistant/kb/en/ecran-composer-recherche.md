You are on the search filters ("Filter trips" or "Filter parcels"). Set "WHERE", "WHEN" and "AROUND ME", plus weight and price, contents, stops, payment methods and urgency for a trip, or maximum weight, size and "For my trips" for a parcel. "Clear all" resets everything, and "Search" shows the number of results and starts the search.
