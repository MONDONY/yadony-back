You are on the search filters ("Filter trips" or "Filter parcels"). Set "WHERE", "WHEN" and "AROUND ME", then weight and price and the quick filters (Urgent, 23 kg suitcase, GP Pro, Verified identity) for a trip; "More filters" expands contents, transport, rating, stops and payment methods, or maximum weight, size and "For my trips" for a parcel. "Clear all" resets everything, and "Search" shows the number of results and starts the search.
