A traveler is either an Individual or a GP Pro. The professional is the GP Pro: a single badge, validated by the Yadony team.
Individual: you travel for personal reasons and it is not your job. You tick the box that says so. Senders see "Individual" on your profile; consumer law does not apply to the agreement made with you.
GP Pro: you fill in the GP Pro form (business name, company number such as SIRET, NINEA or RCCM, country), with a verified identity. The team reviews and decides. Meanwhile you publish as an individual. Business name and number are visible to signed-in senders; the public page opened without an account never shows the number.
The status question appears when you tap "Publish", until you have answered. "Later" publishes the trip anyway. You can also reopen it from Edit profile, "Traveler status" row.
Monthly limit: the team can set, account by account, a maximum number of trips published per month. There is none by default. Once the limit is reached, publishing is blocked before the trip is created: as an Individual you wait for the 1st of next month, or you apply for GP Pro. A validated GP Pro is never limited. Drafts do not count.
Become an individual again: one button and a confirmation. The GP Pro badge, the business name, the number and the country are removed; to get the badge back you must fill in the form again.
