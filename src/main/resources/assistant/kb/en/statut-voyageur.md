title: Traveler status, individual or professional

---
Before you publish a trip, Yadony asks whether you travel as an individual or as a professional. Nothing is preselected: you choose, then you confirm.
Individual: you travel for personal reasons and it is not your job. You tick the box that says so. Senders see "Individual" on your trip and on your profile. For them, consumer law does not apply to the agreement made with you.
Professional: you carry parcels as part of a business. You give the business name, the company number (SIRET, NINEA, RCCM or other) and the country of registration. Signed-in senders can see this on your trip and on your profile. The public page opened without an account shows only the status and the business name, never the number.
You can change your status at any time from your profile. Switching to Individual erases the business name, the number and the country.
This declaration is separate from the GP Pro badge: the badge means a profile reviewed by the Yadony team, not a professional.
Yadony can make this declaration mandatory to publish a trip. Publishing is then refused until something is declared; saving a draft is always possible.
