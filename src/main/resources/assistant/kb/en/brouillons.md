When you leave a publishing form, the app offers "Save draft" so you can resume later on this phone, at the step where you were.
When you start a publication, the "To finish" section lists the drafts you can resume.
Your unpublished requests are also in My requests, Drafts filter; your trips in My trips, Drafts filter.
The number of drafts is limited: once the limit is reached, publish or delete a draft.
