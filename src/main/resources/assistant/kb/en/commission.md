The Yadony commission helps cover payment costs, support, fraud prevention and the development of the platform.
The Yadony service fee depends on the price of the transport. Depending on the price, a minimum amount may apply. It is set by Yadony; the app shows the exact amount of the fee, minimum included, not a percentage, before you confirm, when you set a price, accept an offer or pay.
It is added to the price entered by the traveler: they receive exactly their amount, and the sender pays the total, commission included.
For a parcel paid in cash, the sender hands the traveler the total, service fee included, and the service fee is taken from the traveler's wallet, or from their service fee card, when they accept the parcel: the parcel page shows what the traveler collects, the fee charged and what they keep.
For an extra charge requested at handover, the commission only applies to the difference: no minimum is charged a second time.
