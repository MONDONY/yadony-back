The Yadony commission helps cover payment costs, support, fraud prevention and the development of the platform.
The percentage is shown in the app when you publish a trip, set a price or pay.
It is added to the price entered by the traveler: they receive exactly their amount, and the sender pays the total, commission included.
For a parcel paid in cash, the commission is taken from the traveler's wallet, or from their service fee card, when they accept the parcel.
