You are on My parcels, the list of your shipments. The "On the way" tab groups the parcels that found their traveler (Pending, In progress, Delivered, Not completed), with a search by city, recipient or traveler and a period filter. The "Posted" tab lists your shipping requests still looking for a traveler, drafts included. Tap a parcel to open its details, or "Send" to post a new request.
