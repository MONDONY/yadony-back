Traveler tracking is an optional bonus: it reassures the sender, but it never blocks a parcel, a payment or a delivery.
It gives an approximate position, never an exact one: the nearest city and the share of the route already covered. There is no map.
As the traveler, the app asks for location permission "while using the app" only, once, with a clear explanation sheet. You can decline, and your parcels travel normally.
A position is taken at only three moments: when you tap "I'm leaving", when you tap "I've arrived", and when you open the app while your trip is being tracked (from 30 minutes before departure until delivery). Nothing runs in the background.
Two notifications remind you of these buttons: 30 minutes before departure, and 30 minutes after the arrival time you entered (none if you did not enter one).
As the sender, your parcel's page shows the status ("On the way", "Arrived", "Tracking not enabled by the traveler" or "last position is old"), a progress bar, the nearest city, the history of cities crossed between the scan of your parcel and its delivery, and the photos of the steps.
A recipient without an account only sees the latest position on the public tracking page. No SMS or message is sent to recipients without an account.
Limits: if the traveler does not open the app, the position may be old. "Tracking not enabled by the traveler" means they do not share their position, not that something is wrong with the parcel.
