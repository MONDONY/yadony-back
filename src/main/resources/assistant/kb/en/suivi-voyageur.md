Traveler tracking is an optional bonus: it reassures the sender, but it never blocks a parcel, a payment or a delivery.
It gives an approximate position, never an exact one: the nearest city and the share of the route already covered. Its position is not drawn on a map.
As the traveler, the app asks for location permission "while using the app" only, once, with a clear explanation sheet. You can decline, and your parcels travel normally.
A position is taken at only three moments: when you tap "I'm leaving", when you tap "Arrived at destination", and when you open the app while your trip is being tracked (from 30 minutes before departure until "Arrived at destination" or delivery). Nothing runs in the background.
Once at least one parcel has been scanned, two notifications remind you of these buttons: 30 minutes before departure, and 30 minutes after the arrival time you entered (none if you did not enter one).
As the sender, open the "Tracking" tile in the "Your shipment" section (before handover, it is called "Places"). It shows the status ("On the way", "Arrived", "Tracking not enabled by the traveler" or "last position is old"), the traveler's approximate position if the traveler shares it (progress bar, nearest city), the map of the current step and the addresses. In the window, "See all steps and photos" opens the steps, the photos and the history of cities crossed between the scan and the delivery.
A recipient without an account only sees the latest position on the public tracking page. No SMS or message is sent to recipients without an account.
Once the last parcel of the trip is delivered, the traveler's approximate positions are deleted.
Limits: if the traveler does not open the app, the position may be old. "Tracking not enabled by the traveler" means they do not share their position, not that something is wrong with the parcel.
