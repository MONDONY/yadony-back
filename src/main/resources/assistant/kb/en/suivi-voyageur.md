Traveler tracking is an optional bonus: it reassures the sender, but it never blocks a parcel or a payment.
Approximate position, never exact: the nearest city and the share of the route covered.
As the traveler, the app asks once for location permission "while using the app" only. You can decline, and your parcels travel normally.
A position is taken at only three moments: when you tap "I'm leaving", when you tap "Arrived at destination", and when you open the app while your trip is being tracked (from 30 minutes before departure until "Arrived at destination" or delivery).
On the trip screen, "I'm leaving" appears under "To do" from 30 minutes before departure, if a parcel has been handed to you. "Arrived at destination" is the sticky bottom button, but only when the trip is under way (at departure time, or as soon as a parcel is scanned in transit) and a parcel has been handed to you or is in transit. During the handover step, before departure, it does not exist; after departure time, it can appear up to an hour late.
If a parcel has been scanned, two notifications remind you of these buttons: 30 minutes before departure, and 30 minutes after the arrival time you entered.
As the sender, open the "Tracking" tile in the "Your shipment" section (before handover, it is called "Places"). It shows the status ("On the way", "Arrived", "Tracking not enabled by the traveler" or "last position is old"), the traveler's approximate position if the traveler shares it (progress bar, nearest city). "See all steps and photos" opens the step details.
A recipient without an account only sees the latest position on the public tracking page. No SMS is sent to recipients without an account.
Once the last parcel of the trip is delivered, the traveler's positions are deleted.
Limits: if the traveler does not open the app, the position may be old. "Tracking not enabled by the traveler" means they do not share their position, not a parcel problem.
