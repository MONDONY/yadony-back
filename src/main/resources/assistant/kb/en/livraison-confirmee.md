On arrival, the traveler declares their arrival, then hands the parcel to the recipient: they take a photo of the parcel and enter the 6-digit pickup code the recipient gives them (not the DON-… tracking number).
Confirmation is only possible after the trip has departed.
Once delivery is confirmed, the payment is released to the traveler and the sender can rate the traveler.
If the recipient doesn't show up, see "Absent recipient".
