Search tab: "I'm sending a parcel" lists travelers, "I'm traveling" lists parcels to carry. The screen opens on "I'm traveling" only if you chose "Travel with parcels"; the selector switches mode at any time, and Settings › "What I do on Yadony" sets the mode for the next launch. Full-screen map: tap a price to see the trip. "List" shows the results. The location button turns on "Near me" (5 to 200 km, "Radius" chip). The search bar sets cities and filters, "+" publishes, the bookmark opens your favorites.
