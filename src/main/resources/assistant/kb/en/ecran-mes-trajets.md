You are on My trips: all your trips, filtered by "All", "Drafts", "Active", "Completed" and "Canceled", with a search by destination. Tap a trip to open its page, or pin it to keep it at the top of the list. "+ New" posts a new trip. To delete a canceled trip, swipe it to the left.
