When you open the app, on some tabs, or before publishing a trip or sending a parcel, a window may offer a short video presenting Yadony.
"Watch the video" plays it in the app; "Get started" closes the window and lets you carry on (to the form if you were publishing a trip or sending a parcel).
If you swipe the window away without touching a button, it may come back later, a few times at most. Once you have answered, it does not come back, unless a new video is published.
You can find the video and the other tutorials at any time in Profile › Help › Social media & tutorials.
