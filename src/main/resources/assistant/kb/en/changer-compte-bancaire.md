Your bank account is stored with Stripe, Yadony's payment provider. To change it, tap "Change bank account": your Stripe space opens in the browser, Stripe sends you a code by SMS or email, then you change the IBAN in the payout settings.
The new account is used for the next transfers; a transfer already in progress goes to the old one.
If your Stripe sign-up isn't finished, the app first offers to complete it.
Do this too after a "Bank transfer failed" notification: the money goes back to your Stripe account and leaves with the next transfer to the new account.
