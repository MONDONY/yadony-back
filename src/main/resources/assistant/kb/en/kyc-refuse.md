The "Verification failed" screen gives the reason: expired or unsupported document, unreadable document, selfie not matching the document photo, or consent declined.
Accepted documents are the national ID card, driver's license, passport and residence permit or residence card.
The app invites you to start again: tap "Retry verification" with a sharp, complete, well-lit document; for the selfie, stand in a well-lit place, without glasses or headwear.
If the camera is blocked, allow it for Yadony in your phone settings, then come back: the verification resumes.
Verification is for adults only. If the problem persists, contact support.
If you start without finishing, or if verification fails for a reason you can fix (photo, expired document), Yadony sends you up to 3 reminders, by notification and by e-mail, within 7 days. The link at the bottom of the e-mail turns them off for good.
