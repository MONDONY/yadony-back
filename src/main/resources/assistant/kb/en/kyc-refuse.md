The "Verification failed" screen gives the reason: expired or unsupported document, unreadable document, selfie not matching the document photo, or consent declined.
Tap "Retry verification" with a sharp, complete, well-lit document; for the selfie, stand in a well-lit place, without glasses or headwear.
If the camera is blocked, allow it for Yadony in your phone settings, then come back: the verification resumes.
Verification is for adults only. If the problem persists, contact support.
