To report someone, open their profile ("More options" menu) or the conversation, then tap "Report".
Choose a reason: harassment, fake profile, scam attempt, prohibited item, misleading information, inappropriate content, spam, payment issue or other.
Optionally add an explanation and up to 4 screenshots, then send: our team reviews the report.
A trip or a request can also be reported from its page, and you can block the person to stop seeing them.
