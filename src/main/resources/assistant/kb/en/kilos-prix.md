For capacity, choose a 23 or 32 kg suitcase, a custom number of kilos or unlimited capacity sold by the kilo.
For the price, choose "By the kilo" or "Price grid + kilo": the grid sets a price per item and is managed in Activity › My price grid, for all your trips.
Yadony adds its commission to the price you enter: you receive exactly your amount, and the screen shows what you get and what the sender pays.
Turn on "I accept price proposals" so senders can negotiate.
