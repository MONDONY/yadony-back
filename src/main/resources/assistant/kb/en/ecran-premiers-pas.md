You are on the end of sign-up screen, "Where to start?". Tap "Send a parcel" to find a traveler or post your shipping request, "Travel with parcels" to publish your trip, or "I'm just looking" to explore. "Later" takes you to the home screen without choosing anything. Your choice does not lock you in: the home screen opens on "I'm sending a parcel", or on "I'm traveling" if you chose "Travel with parcels", and you may switch between the two at any moment with the selector on the home screen.
