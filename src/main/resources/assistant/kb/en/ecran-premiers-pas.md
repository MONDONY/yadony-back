You are on the end of sign-up screen: your account is ready. Choose where to start: "I'm traveling" to publish your trip, or "I'm sending a parcel" to find a traveler or post your shipping request. Depending on your profile, the screen may instead show the trips or parcels on your route, or offer you an alert. "Later, go to home" takes you to the home screen, and you can always do the other one later.
