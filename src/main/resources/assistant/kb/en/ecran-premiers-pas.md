You are on the end of sign-up screen, "What would you like to start with?". Tap "Send a parcel" to find a traveler or post your shipping request, "Travel with parcels" to publish your trip, or "I'll just look around" to explore. "Later" takes you to the home screen without choosing anything. Your choice does not lock you in: the home screen opens on "I'm sending a parcel", or on "I'm traveling" if you chose "Travel with parcels", and you may switch between the two at any moment with the selector on the home screen.
