In a conversation, tap the call icon then choose "Yadony call" (free, without showing your number) or "Phone call" (with your plan).
During the call, you can mute the microphone, turn on the speaker, minimize the call or hang up.
If the microphone is denied, allow it in your phone settings.
To see a call while the phone is locked, allow calls on the lock screen; missed calls are set in Notifications.
