To validate a step, open the Tracking tab in "Validate a step" mode, choose your trip and scan the parcel's QR: each scan validates the next step (pickup, optional transit, arrival).
If the QR is unreadable, tap "QR unreadable? Enter the number" and enter the DON-… tracking number; a photo of the parcel is then required.
Without network, the scan is saved and sent as soon as the connection returns (see Offline scans).
If an extra charge is waiting for the sender's answer or payment, the handover scan is blocked until they answer. It is also blocked as long as your report of the sender's no-show has not been lifted.
If the camera is denied, allow it in your phone settings.
