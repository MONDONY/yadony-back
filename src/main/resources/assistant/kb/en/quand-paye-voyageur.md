By card, the money is secured at acceptance and held by Yadony; the traveler is only paid once the recipient has confirmed delivery.
They then receive an automatic transfer to the bank account they connected, or a payout to their mobile money account if they chose that method.
In cash, they are paid by hand at handover, and only the Yadony commission goes through the app.
In case of a dispute, the payment stays frozen while the Yadony team reviews it.
You can follow your payouts in My money.
