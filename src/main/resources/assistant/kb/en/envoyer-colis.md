There are two ways to send a parcel.
Either you look for a trip in the Search tab, open it and make a request or offer a price to the traveler.
Or you publish your request with the Publish button of the Search tab, then "Send a parcel" (or from the "I'm sending" tile of the Activity tab): matching travelers send you offers.
Your identity must be verified to send a parcel.
A traveler carries the parcel in their luggage, card payment is secured and released on confirmed delivery, and tracking is done by QR from handover to reception.
