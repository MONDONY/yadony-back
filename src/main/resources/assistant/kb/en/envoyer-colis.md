There are two ways to send a parcel.
Either you look for a trip in the Search tab, open it and make a request or offer a price to the traveler.
Or you publish your request with the Publish button of the Search tab, then "Send a parcel" (or from the "I'm sending" tile of the Activity tab): matching travelers send you offers.
Your identity must be verified to send a parcel.
Weight and budget depend on the mode. By road and by boat, there is no 32 kg cap (up to 999,999 kg) and no budget cap set by Yadony; only a technical safeguard refuses a budget above 1,000,000 in the request's currency (about 1,500 € in CFA francs). By air and "Any": 32 kg and 560 € at most.
The parcel size (S to XL) is optional: it rules out travelers whose max size is smaller.
For boat, give the parcel's dimensions (length, width, height in cm, 400 at most each): Yadony works out the volume in m³, which sets the price and is required to publish the request (a draft can do without). For "Any", the dimensions are optional, but all three if you give any.
A boat trip announces its arrival as a date range and its price per m³; if the traveler announces a delay, you are notified.
A traveler carries the parcel, card payment is secured and released on confirmed delivery, and tracking is done by QR from handover to reception.
