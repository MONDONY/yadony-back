The refund depends on the payment method and when the cancellation happens.
Card: credited back to the method used after processing; mobile money: delay depends on the operator.
If the traveler cancels their trip, or if the drop-off deadline has passed, the sender is fully refunded automatically.
If the sender is the one who cancels an already accepted parcel, or does not show up at handover, a cancellation fee may be kept from their refund, depending on the parcel's date and the time left before departure (scale in the "Cancellation fee" article). When the sender cancels themselves, the app shows the exact amount before they confirm (preview available); if it says no fee applies, they are refunded in full. With no amount (preview unavailable), the scale applies at cancellation. When their no-show at handover is confirmed, no amount is shown beforehand: if a fee applies, a notification tells them the amount kept and the amount refunded; if no fee is announced, they are refunded in full. The traveler is never charged when they cancel.
In cash, Yadony does not hold the funds and cannot refund automatically; if the shipment is cancelled or the parcel refused, the service fee paid by the traveler is always given back to them, even if they miss the appointment.
An extra charge paid by card or mobile money is also fully refunded if undelivered; in cash, the extra charge service fee is given back to the traveler.
A parcel change paid by card or mobile money is refunded with the parcel: both transactions are returned together if the parcel isn't delivered. If the traveler declines it, if it expires or if you cancel it, the amount held on your card is released; if you had paid it and the traveler reports the addition wasn't brought at handover, it is refunded to you. In cash, the change's service fee is given back to the traveler.
Once delivery is confirmed and the money is paid to the traveler, no automatic refund is possible: the team can help if there is a problem.
