The refund depends on the payment method and when the cancellation happens.
A card payment is credited back to the method used after processing, and for mobile money the delay depends on the operator.
If the traveler cancels their trip, or if the drop-off deadline has passed, the sender is fully refunded automatically.
In cash, Yadony does not hold the funds and cannot refund automatically; if the shipment is cancelled or the parcel refused, the service fee paid by the traveler is always given back to them, even if they miss the appointment.
An extra charge paid by card or mobile money is also fully refunded if the parcel isn't delivered; in cash, the extra charge service fee is given back to the traveler.
A parcel change paid by card or mobile money is refunded with the parcel: both transactions are returned together if the parcel isn't delivered. If the traveler declines the change, if it expires or if you cancel it, the amount held on your card is released; if you had already paid it and the traveler reports that the addition wasn't brought at handover, it is refunded to you. In cash, the change's service fee is given back to the traveler.
Once delivery is confirmed and the money is paid to the traveler, no automatic refund is possible any more: the team can help if there is a problem.
