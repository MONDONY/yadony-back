You are on a parcel addressed to you. If it is waiting for your answer, tap "Yes, it's for me" to follow it and get your pickup code, or "It's not for me". Then the screen shows the parcel's step, your pickup code ("Copy the code") and "Show the parcel QR" to the traveler. The code is secret: only give it at handover. You can also "Message the traveler" or "View tracking".
