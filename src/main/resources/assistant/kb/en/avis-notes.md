Once the parcel is delivered, you can rate the other person from 1 to 5 stars, with an optional comment: "Rate the traveler" for the sender, "Rate the sender" for the traveler.
Your received reviews are in Profile › My reputation › Reviews received, with the breakdown by stars, and you can report one.
Your rating and reviews also appear on your public profile, in Profile › My reputation › My public profile.
