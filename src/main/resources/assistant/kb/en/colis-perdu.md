Yadony does not automatically cover the loss of a parcel.
After investigation, a capped refund may be granted if all conditions are met: card payment made within Yadony, no payment or agreement outside the platform, drop-off and handover QR codes used, dispute opened within 15 days of the expected date, content complying with allowed items.
Any decision remains subject to approval by the Yadony team.
The cap is shown in the app with the "See conditions" link.
