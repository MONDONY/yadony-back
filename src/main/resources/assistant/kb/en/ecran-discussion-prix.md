You are in a price discussion on a parcel request, between the sender and a traveler. When it is your turn, tap "Accept" (the amount is shown on it), "Counter-offer" or "Decline"; "Send a reminder" nudges the other person when available. Once agreed, the bottom of the screen shows the next step: the traveler picks "Link a trip to this offer" or "Create a dedicated trip", the sender taps "Complete & pay", and in cash the traveler taps "Pay the service fee". The ⋯ menu offers "End the negotiation".
