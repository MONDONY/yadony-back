Open Profile › My benefits › Referrals to copy your code or invitation link and share it, on WhatsApp for example.
The reward unlocks when your friend's first parcel is delivered: you then choose an amount credited to your Yadony balance, or a discount code for your next shipment, valid for 6 months.
This choice is final.
If you were the one invited, enter your friend's referral code in Profile › My benefits › "I have a referral code".
