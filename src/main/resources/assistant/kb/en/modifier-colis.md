As the sender, once your parcel is accepted and before handover, you can enlarge it: parcel details › "Change my parcel". You can only add, never remove: kilos, within the kilos still free on the trip, and/or items from the traveler's price grid. The original parcel stays paid as before: you only pay the difference, service fee included.
The traveler has 48 hours to accept or decline, less if departure is close (the option disappears about 2 h 15 before departure, or 2 h before the drop-off deadline). With no answer, the request expires; you can cancel it until they answer. Only one request open at a time, at most 2 accepted changes per parcel; the handover scan is blocked while it waits.
Pay with any method the traveler accepts. Card: amount held when you send, charged on acceptance, released on decline, expiry or cancellation. Mobile money: nothing when you send, you pay after acceptance from the parcel details, otherwise the change lapses. Cash: extra amount handed over in person; on acceptance, the traveler pays the Yadony service fee of the change. Too little balance: acceptance refused with nothing charged, the request stays open; declined card: back to awaiting an answer. They can retry until the deadline (balance, another card); without bank confirmation within 30 minutes (card or fee), the request is cancelled.
As the traveler, answer from the parcel details. If the addition isn't brought at handover, tap "The extra kilos are not there": change cancelled, kilos back to the trip, cash service fee returned, sender refunded if they paid online, original parcel at the agreed price.
Outside cash, the parcel and its change are paid out together to the traveler after delivery, and refunded together if the parcel isn't delivered.
Parcel born from a shipping request (negotiated price): no change possible, ask the traveler to adjust the price (extra charge) until handover.
