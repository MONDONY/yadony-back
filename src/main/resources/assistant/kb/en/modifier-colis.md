As the sender, once your parcel is accepted and before handover, you can enlarge it: parcel details › "Change my parcel". You can only add, never remove: kilos, within the kilos still free on the trip and at the trip's price per kilo, and/or items from the traveler's price grid. The original parcel stays paid as before: you only pay the difference, service fee included.
The traveler has 48 hours to accept or decline, less if departure is close (within 2 hours of departure or of the drop-off deadline, the option disappears). With no answer, the request expires; you can cancel it until they answer. Only one request open at a time, at most 2 accepted changes per parcel; the handover scan is blocked while it waits.
You pay with a method the traveler accepts, possibly different from the parcel's. Card: the amount is held when you send (without bank confirmation within 30 minutes, the request is cancelled), then charged on acceptance; a decline, expiry or cancellation releases it. Mobile money: nothing when you send, you pay after acceptance from the parcel details, otherwise the change lapses. Cash: you hand the extra amount over in person, and the traveler pays the Yadony service fee of the change on acceptance.
As the traveler, you see your extra earnings and answer from the parcel details. If the sender doesn't bring the addition at handover, tap "The extra kilos are not there": the change is cancelled, the kilos go back to the trip, your cash service fee is returned, the sender is refunded if they paid online, and the original parcel goes ahead at the agreed price.
The parcel and its change are paid out together to the traveler after delivery, and refunded together if the parcel isn't delivered.
Parcel born from a shipping request (negotiated price): no change possible, the traveler uses the price adjustment (extra charge) until handover.
