Receipts (payment, payout, top-up), team decisions (dispute, no-show, moderation, identity) and key parcel steps (booking, delivery, cancellation, return) are also sent to you by email if your account has an address. Reminders, tips and the daily summary of your alerts arrive by email instead of a notification. In Settings › Notifications, you can turn off "Summaries" and "Alerts and tips" emails; receipts and decisions are always sent.
After a search with no result, Yadony may write to you when a matching trip is posted ("Based on your searches"). To stop them, tap "Stop these suggestions" at the bottom of the email and confirm. Turning off "Alerts and tips" also stops these emails, and turning off "New trips" stops these suggestions (notification and email).
Nothing is sent if your account has no email address: add one in Profile › Edit profile › Email.
