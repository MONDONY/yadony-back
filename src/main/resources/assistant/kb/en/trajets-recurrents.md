There are two ways to repeat a trip. The series keeps the template's mode, capacity, size and max weight; by air, 40 kg at most per series.
In Activity › Trip templates, open a template then "Schedule the recurring trip": choose the repeat days and the departure time, then "Make it a recurring trip" so upcoming trips are published automatically.
Or, after publishing a trip, tap "Make this trip recurring" and pick other dates (12 at most): the same trip is prepared for each one, published or as a draft.
This entry is also in the ⋮ menu of an existing trip, published or completed. It only exists for a direct trip: a multi-leg trip cannot be repeated. If you have reached your monthly trip limit, you can always save a draft: only publishing is blocked, until next month or until you get GP Pro status.
