There are two ways to repeat a trip.
In Activity › Trip templates, open a template then "Schedule the recurring trip": choose the repeat days and the departure time, then "Make it a recurring trip" so upcoming trips are published automatically.
Or, after publishing a trip, tap "Make this trip recurring" and pick other dates (12 at most): the same trip is prepared for each one, published or as a draft.
