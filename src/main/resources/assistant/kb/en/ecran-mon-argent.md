You are on My money: it shows where the money for your parcels stands. As a traveler, the "Upcoming" card, "Upcoming payouts" and "Recently paid out" show what will be paid to you and what already has been, and "All my trips" breaks it down by trip. As a sender, "My shipments" follows the money for your paid parcels. Tap a line to open the parcel, and "My Yadony balance" for your wallet.
