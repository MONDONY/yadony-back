The pickup code is a secret 6-digit code: the recipient only gives it to the traveler at handover, not before.
The sender sees it in their shipment details and passes it to their recipient; they do not give it to the traveler.
If it is blocked after too many wrong attempts, or expired, the sender generates a new one with "Regenerate the code": the limit is 5 regenerations, then the button re-enables itself automatically after a delay.
Do not confuse it with the DON-… tracking number, which identifies the parcel and is not a handover code.
