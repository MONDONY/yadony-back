The pickup code is a secret 6-digit code. It does not exist at acceptance: it is created when the traveler scans the parcel at departure (the handover). The sender then receives the "Send the pickup code" notification and sees the code in their shipment details.
It is up to the sender to give it to their recipient: "Let them know" button on the parcel, a WhatsApp message with the tracking link, the code and an invitation to install the app. If the recipient has the app and their link is confirmed, they see their code in the reception screen and get the "Your parcel is on its way" notification ("Your pickup code is ready"). A recipient without the app receives nothing from Yadony: only the sender's message.
On arrival, the traveler must not hand over the parcel without this code: the app offers no way to confirm delivery without it. They are warned in their arrival notification and on the delivery confirmation screen. The recipient gives their code to the traveler at handover, not before. The sender does not give it to the traveler.
If it is blocked after too many wrong attempts, or expired, the sender generates a new one with "Regenerate the code" (the traveler can ask for one): the limit is 5 regenerations, then the button re-enables itself automatically after a delay.
If you are both the sender and the recipient, you collect the parcel yourself: keep your code, the "Let them know" step does not apply.
Do not confuse it with the DON-… tracking number, which identifies the parcel and is not a handover code.
