The pickup code is a secret 6-digit code, created when the traveler scans the parcel at departure, not at acceptance. The sender is notified and gives it to the recipient ("Let them know", WhatsApp message). A recipient with the app sees it there; one without receives nothing from Yadony.
On arrival, the traveler hands over nothing without the code: the app cannot confirm delivery without it.
If it is blocked or expired, the sender generates a new one ("Regenerate the code"; the traveler can ask for one), 5 times at most.
Sender and recipient the same person: you collect the parcel yourself, keep your code.
Not the DON-… tracking number.
