The pickup code is a secret 6-digit code, created at the traveler's departure scan, not at acceptance. The sender gives it to the recipient ("Let them know"); without the app, the recipient receives nothing from Yadony.
The traveler hands over nothing without the code.
Blocked or expired: the sender regenerates it (the traveler can ask), 5 times per 24 hours.
Sender and recipient the same person: keep your code.
Not the DON-… tracking number.
