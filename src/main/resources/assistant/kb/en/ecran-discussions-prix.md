You are on "Price discussions": all your negotiations, on a parcel request or on a trip. Filter with "All", "In progress" or "Completed", or search by traveler or city. Each card shows the stage (proposal, deal, to pay, paid…) and "Your move" when your reply is expected: tap it to open the discussion. A finished discussion can be archived by swiping it, and the "Archived" row lets you find it again.
