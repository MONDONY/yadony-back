You are on your shipping request: the ticket sums up the trip and the budget, and travelers' "Offers received" appear below. Tap "Choose" on the offer you want: it is a single choice, and the other candidates are declined automatically. You then "Pay" to secure their spot. The bottom button changes with the step ("Edit", "Share", "Pay", "Track my parcel"), and "More actions" lets you "Unpublish", "Duplicate the request" or "Cancel the request".
