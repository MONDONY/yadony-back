GP Pro is the badge for regular travelers and businesses that carry parcels every week or nearly so.
To apply: Me › ☰ › Become a GP Pro. Your identity must be verified first.
You tell us how often you travel and, if you like, a business name. Documents (business registration, tickets, social media page) are optional.
The Yadony team reviews your profile and decides. No turnaround time is guaranteed. You get the answer by notification, and by e-mail if your account has one.
Your documents are deleted as soon as the decision is made.
The badge appears on your trips and your profile, and senders can filter for GP Pros. It brings no service fee discount and no extra listings.
The team can remove the badge, with a reason. After a refusal or a removal, you can apply again right away.
Kilo Pro no longer exists: GP Pro replaces it.
