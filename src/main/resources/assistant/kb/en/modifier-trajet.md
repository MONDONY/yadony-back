The trip's "Edit" button (sticky button at the bottom, or the ⋮ menu) is only offered for a draft or a published trip that has received no request. As soon as it gets a first request, or a parcel is accepted, it disappears and the trip can no longer be edited (the server refuses). In the "Route and places" and "Conditions" windows of "Your trip", "Edit" only appears if the trip can be edited; the "Payments" window has none.
Under "To do", the "Add card to this trip" tile (or "Activate card payments" if your account is not ready) only shows for a cash-only trip that the server would accept to edit: no parcel accepted or on its way. As soon as a parcel is accepted, it disappears.
A draft goes live with the sticky "Post" button.
To change the date of a trip that already has a request, use "Reschedule" (⋮ menu), possible twice at most.
"Unpublish" (⋮ menu) puts a published trip back in your drafts, as long as it has received no request or negotiation.
"Delete" (⋮ menu) is possible for a draft, a canceled trip or a published trip with no request. A full trip cannot be deleted: contact support. With an accepted parcel, cancel it first.
"Share the poster" (bottom button or ⋮ menu) offers a poster to find senders.
