The trip's "Edit" button (sticky button at the bottom, or the ⋮ menu) is only offered for a draft or a published trip that has received no request. As soon as it gets a first request, or a parcel is accepted, it disappears and the trip can no longer be edited (the server refuses). In the "Route and places" and "Conditions" windows of "Your trip", "Edit" only appears if the trip can be edited; the "Payments" window has none.
Under "To do", the "Add card to this trip" action (or "Activate card payments" if your account is not ready) only shows for a cash-only trip that the server would accept to edit: no parcel accepted or on its way. As soon as a parcel is accepted, it disappears. It is optional: it never becomes the sticky bottom button.
A draft goes live with "Post", the top action under "To do", which is also the sticky bottom button.
To change the date of a trip that already has a request, use "Reschedule" (⋮ menu), possible twice at most.
"Unpublish" (⋮ menu) puts a published trip back in your drafts, as long as it has received no request or negotiation; as soon as a parcel or a request exists, "Unpublish" is refused.
"Hide the trip" (⋮ menu, offered when the trip carries parcels) removes the trip from search and public lists without changing its status or touching the parcels: ongoing parcels continue normally, but no new request, offer or negotiation is possible. "Show the trip again" (same menu) puts it back in circulation.
"Delete" (⋮ menu) is possible for a draft, a canceled trip or a published trip with no request. A full trip cannot be deleted: contact support. With an accepted parcel, cancel it first.
"Share the poster" (bottom button when nothing is to do, or ⋮ menu) offers a poster to find senders.
