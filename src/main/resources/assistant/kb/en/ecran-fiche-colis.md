You are on a parcel's details. Ticket at the top (tracking; QR and pickup code once handed over), then "To do", the other person, the payment or the earnings. Recipient by identifier: pending, confirmed or declined. Sender: "Your shipment" as tiles, bottom button "Show the drop-off QR". Traveler: "Your trip" as tiles (Places with the drop-off deadline, Parcel and recipient, More details), bottom: "Decline" or "Accept", "Confirm my presence", "Scan the parcel QR", "Confirm the drop-off". "Options" menu: contact, report, cancel, delete, "Scan the transit".
