You are on a parcel's details. Sender: at the top, the ticket (parcel QR, pickup code, tracking number), then "To do" when an answer is expected, the traveler, the payment, and "Your shipment" as tiles (Let {name} know, Places then Tracking after handover, Parcel and recipient, More details) that open at the bottom of the screen. The bottom button follows the step ("Pay for my shipment", "Show the drop-off QR", "Track my parcel"). Traveler: your earnings, "Accept" or "Decline", then "Scan the parcel QR" and "Confirm the drop-off". The "Options" menu contacts, reports or cancels.
