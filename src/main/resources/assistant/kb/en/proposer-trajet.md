From the Search tab, tap the Publish button then "Publish a trip".
From Activity, the "I'm traveling" tile also offers "Post a trip" while you have no trip; after that, "Active trips" opens My trips and its "+ New" button.
You first accept your commitments as a traveler: carry the parcel yourself, respect the date and itinerary, scan the QR at handover and delivery, accept only authorized content.
Your identity must be verified.
When you tap "Publish", Yadony may ask whether you travel as an individual or as a GP Pro ("Later" publishes anyway). If the team set a monthly trip limit on your account and you have reached it, publishing is blocked until the month ends, except for a validated GP Pro. You can always save a draft: only publishing is blocked, until next month or until you get GP Pro status.
The form has up to five steps: "Mode" (if several modes are offered: Air or Road), "Trip" (cities, dates, drop-off deadline), "Capacity" (handover places and capacity), "Price" (currency at the top, price, payment methods) then "Terms" (optional: "Skip"). The multi-leg trip is set at the bottom of "Terms". When editing, the mode is changed at the top of "Trip". Then publish, or save as a draft.
By road, arrival can be up to 14 days after departure ("Other date", no arrival time). Capacity has no suitcase: "Free kilos" or "Custom", up to several tons. "Max parcel size" (S bag, M box, L suitcase or chair, XL furniture; indicative) and "Max weight per parcel" are optional. By air: 999 kg at most per trip.
The drop-off deadline is shown in the departure city's local time. After it, nobody can make a request on your trip anymore.
Once the trip is published, the confirmation screen offers "See parcels for this trip": it opens the Search tab in "I'm traveling" mode, on the parcels that match your trips. "View my trip" opens its details and "Share my poster" opens the poster to share.
