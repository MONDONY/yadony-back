Your balance is shown in Profile › Money (the "Balance" card), which opens My wallet.
It is used to pay the Yadony commission on requests paid in cash.
You can top it up, request a refund and follow your requests, and the history lists every transaction.
Each balance stays in its own currency with no conversion, and the "Active wallet" receives your card top-ups and sets the currency of your new listings.
