Your balance is shown in Profile › Money (the "Balance" card), which opens My wallet.
It is used to pay the Yadony service fee when the sender pays the traveler in cash. It does not pay for sending a parcel, which is paid by card or mobile money (PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay are coming soon).
You can top it up, request a refund and follow your requests, and the history lists every transaction.
Each balance stays in its own currency: nothing is converted until you spend. The "Active wallet" receives your card top-ups and sets the currency of your new listings.
To pay a service fee, Yadony first takes from the wallet in the parcel's currency, then from your other wallets, biggest to smallest, converted at today's rate. If together they are not enough, nothing is taken. A wallet is set aside while a refund is in progress on it, or when its exchange rate is out of date (more than 48 hours old, CFA franc excepted).
A refund always goes back in the currency of the top-up, for the exact amount that remains.
