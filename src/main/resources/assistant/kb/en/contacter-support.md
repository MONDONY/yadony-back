Open Profile › Help › Contact support: the answer usually arrives within 24 hours.
The Support page shows the frequently asked questions and your tickets.
Tap "New request", choose a category (Account, Identity verification, Payment, Trip, Parcel, Delivery or Other), a subject and a message describing the problem; you can attach images to your messages.
Follow the answer in My tickets; a resolved ticket is not reopened, open a new one for another problem.
