The quickest way: in the Messages tab, tap the "Yadony Support" row (Yadony logo) at the top of the list. It opens the conversation with the team directly, in the same format as your other messages: messages the team writes to you arrive there too. With no open request, the conversation is empty and your first message opens a request.
You can also open Profile › Help › Contact support: the answer usually arrives within 24 hours.
When the team has set its number, the WhatsApp icon at the top of the conversation and the "Write on WhatsApp" card on the Support page open WhatsApp on the conversation with the Yadony team. Without WhatsApp on the phone, a sheet shows the number and offers Call, Send an SMS or Copy.
The Support page then shows the assistant, the frequently asked questions and your tickets.
Tap "New request", choose a category (Account, Identity verification, Payment, Trip, Parcel, Delivery or Other), a subject and a message describing the problem; you can attach images to your messages.
Follow the answer in My tickets; a resolved ticket is not reopened, open a new one for another problem.
