You are on a public profile. Opened from Profile, it is yours as others see it ("What others see"); otherwise it belongs to another member. It shows their deliveries, rating, reply time, verifications, bio, languages, badges and recent reviews. On another member's profile, "Follow" lets you know about their new trips, and the "More options" menu (⋯) lets you report or block them.
