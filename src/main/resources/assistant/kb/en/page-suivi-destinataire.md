The recipient does not need an account: they follow the parcel in real time with the tracking link you send them.
From Activity › My parcels › your shipment, tap "Share tracking" or use the "Let [name] know" card to send it by WhatsApp.
You can choose to show or remove the pickup code on the tracking page.
If the recipient has Yadony, they can also find the parcel in the Tracking tab, under Receiving.
If the traveler shares their position, the page only shows their latest approximate position (nearest city and progress), without the history. No message is sent automatically to a recipient without an account.
