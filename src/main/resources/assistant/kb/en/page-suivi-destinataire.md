The recipient does not need an account: they follow the parcel in real time with the tracking link you send them.
From Activity › My parcels › your shipment, open the "Let {name} know" tile in "Your shipment" to send it by WhatsApp, or tap "Share tracking" (share icon in the header or "Options" menu).
You can choose to show or remove the pickup code on the tracking page.
If the recipient has Yadony, they can also find the parcel in the Tracking tab, under Receiving.
If the traveler shares their position, the page only shows their latest approximate position (nearest city and progress), without the history. No message is sent automatically to a recipient without an account.
On your side, in "Your shipment", once the request is accepted and before the parcel is handed to the traveler, the "Places" tile shows the map of the current step and, below it, the drop-off and pickup addresses. Once the parcel is handed over, it becomes "Tracking": the traveler's approximate position (if the traveler shares their location), the map of the current step and both addresses. The "See all steps and photos" button at the bottom opens the steps and photos.
