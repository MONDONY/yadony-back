You are on My wallet: your balance pays the Yadony service fee on requests paid in cash, not for sending a parcel. Tap "Top up" to add funds, "Refund" to get back a refundable balance and "Requests" to follow your refunds; the "History" lists every transaction. The "Active wallet" card ("Change" button) switches from one currency to another without converting anything. The "How it works" icon sums it all up.
