You are on My wallet: your balance pays the Yadony service fee on requests paid in cash, not for sending a parcel. The total at the top estimates all your balances at today's rate. Tap "My balances" at the top (or "Change" under the total) to see the money in each currency and choose the total's currency: nothing is converted. "Top up" adds funds, "Refund" gets back a refundable balance, "Requests" follows your refunds; the "History" lists every transaction in its own currency.
