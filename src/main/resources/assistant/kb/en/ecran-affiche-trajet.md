You are on "My poster", your trip's image ready to share. "Share the poster" sends it to the app of your choice, "Copy the caption" and "Copy the link" prepare your post's text, "Save to gallery" keeps the image, and "Post in the Yadony group" shows up when a group is available. Always paste the caption into your post's text: a link written on the image is not clickable.
