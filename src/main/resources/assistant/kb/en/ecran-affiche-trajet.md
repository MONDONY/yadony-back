You are on "My poster" ("Poster" if the listing is not yours), the image ready to share. "Share the poster" sends it to the app of your choice, "Copy the caption" and "Copy the link" prepare your post, "Save to gallery" keeps the image, "Post in the Yadony group" shows up when a group is available. Paste the caption into the post's text: a link written on the image is not clickable. Someone else's trip or parcel can be shared too, with the share icon on its page.
