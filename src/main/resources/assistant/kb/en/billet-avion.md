In Activity, the "Flight tickets" block (See all) lets you search for a flight and shows deals.
Yadony does not sell tickets: you book with our partner, and prices are indicative.
To be notified of a drop, create an alert in My price alerts: choose a route, a period of 31 days at most and your price.
Checks run every 6 hours, and you can have 10 alerts at most.
To hide tickets, turn off "Show flight tickets" in settings.
