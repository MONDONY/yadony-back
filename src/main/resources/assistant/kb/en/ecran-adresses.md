You are on My addresses. The "Drop-off" tab lists your drop-off addresses, the "Delivery" tab your delivery addresses. The + button at the top adds an address to the open tab, and each address's menu offers "Edit", "Set as default" or "Delete". The default address is filled in for your next requests and listings.
