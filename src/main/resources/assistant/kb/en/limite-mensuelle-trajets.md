The monthly limit is set by the Yadony team on some accounts. You can always save a draft: only publishing is blocked, until next month or until your GP Pro status is approved.
When the limit is reached, "Publish" is refused before the trip is created. The counter restarts on the 1st of the next month, and an approved GP Pro is never limited.
To go beyond the limit, apply for GP Pro status from the GP Pro screen. A published trip counts for the month it was published in; deleting it does not free the slot.
