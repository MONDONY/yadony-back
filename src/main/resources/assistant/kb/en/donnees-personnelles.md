Yadony applies security measures to protect your data and does not sell your personal information.
The privacy policy can be read in Profile › ☰ › Settings › Information › Privacy Policy.
Your preferences are managed in Settings, Security & data section: Privacy (profile visibility, phone number) and My data (downloading your data).
See also "Download my personal data" and "Hide my number and set my privacy".
