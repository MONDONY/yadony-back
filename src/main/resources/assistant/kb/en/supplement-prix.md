As the traveler, if the parcel is worth more than planned at handover (for example a phone declared by the kilo while you charge it per item), ask for the difference: parcel details, "Options" › "Request an extra charge". You can do it while the parcel is accepted, or already scanned but before departure, and no longer in the 15 minutes before departure.
Enter the amount for you, between €1 and €300 (or the equivalent in the parcel's currency), choose the reason and take a photo of the parcel: the photo is required. The Yadony service fee is added, calculated on the difference only, and paid by the sender. At most two requests per parcel, one at a time; you can withdraw yours as long as the sender hasn't accepted or started paying.
As the sender, you only pay the difference, service fee included, with the same method as the parcel: card or mobile money from the parcel details, or cash handed to the traveler in person. You can also decline. You have 2 hours to answer, less if departure is close.
While the request awaits your answer or your payment, the handover scan is blocked. If you decline or don't answer in time, the traveler carries the parcel at the agreed price or refuses it with no penalty, and that refusal doesn't count against you.
A paid extra charge is paid out to the traveler with the parcel payment, after delivery. If the parcel isn't delivered, it is fully refunded to the sender; in cash, the traveler gives the extra amount back.
In cash, the extra charge service fee is taken from the traveler as soon as they request it, from their balance or their service fee card (the bank may ask for a confirmation; without confirmation within 30 minutes, the request is cancelled at no cost). The sender pays them back as part of the cash total. It is always given back if the sender declines, if the request expires or is withdrawn, or if the parcel isn't delivered.
