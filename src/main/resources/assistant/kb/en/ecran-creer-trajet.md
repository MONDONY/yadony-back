You are on the trip form, in three steps: "Trip" (cities, date, time, means of transport, drop-off deadline), "Locations & capacity", then "Price & conditions". Tap "Continue" at each step, then "Preview" to check the listing and "Post the listing". The "Save as draft" icon keeps what you entered for later, and "My templates" pre-fills the trip if you have any. The same screen is used to "Edit trip".
