Trip form, in up to five steps: "Mode" (if several modes), "Trip" (cities, dates, "Other date" to arrive later by road, drop-off deadline), "Capacity" (handover, kilos, optional "Max parcel size"), "Price" (currency, price, payments) and "Terms" ("Skip" possible; multi-leg trip at the bottom). "Continue" at each step, then "Preview" and "Post the listing". "Save as draft" keeps what you entered. A template is picked before the form, in "Start from a template?".
