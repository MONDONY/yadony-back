You are on My prices per item, valid on all your trips. Tap "New label", pick an item (or write your own) and enter what you want to receive: Yadony's service fee is added to that amount, and the screen shows what the sender will pay, service fee included. Each label can be edited or deleted from its "Options" menu, and "Reorder" changes their order. These prices are used when you choose "Items + kilo" while posting a trip.
