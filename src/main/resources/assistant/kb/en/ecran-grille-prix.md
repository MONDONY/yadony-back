You are on My price list, valid on all your trips. Tap "New label", pick an item (or write your own) and enter what you want to receive: the screen shows what the sender will pay, Yadony's service fee included. Each label can be edited or deleted from its "Options" menu, and "Reorder" changes their order. The list is used when you choose "Price grid + kilo" while posting a trip.
