When you apply a friend's sponsor code, you receive a personal promo code: 5% off the price you pay for your first shipment, single use, valid for 6 months.
You can apply the code at sign-up (the optional "Referral code" screen right after you create your account, which you can skip), by opening an invitation link, or later in Profile › My benefits › Referrals.
Your code looks like your first name followed by 05 (for example AMINA05), or like YADO-XXXX. A welcome notification tells you what it is.
Enter it when you create your shipment, like any promo code. It is reserved for your account.
You can only be referred once, you cannot use your own code, and an unknown code is refused.
Your sponsor keeps their reward: 5 EUR on their balance or a 5% code, once your first parcel is delivered.
