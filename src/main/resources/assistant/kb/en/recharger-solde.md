In My wallet, tap Top up.
At step 1 of 2, choose the method: credit card, or mobile money (Orange Money, MTN MoMo, Moov) when it is available for your currency.
At step 2, enter the amount; the balance is credited in the currency of the top-up.
The credit appears as soon as the payment is validated; by mobile money, confirm the request on your phone and keep the screen open, confirmation is automatic.
This balance pays the Yadony service fee when the sender pays the traveler in cash, not a parcel.
