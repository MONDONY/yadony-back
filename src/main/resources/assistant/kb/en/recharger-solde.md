In My wallet, tap Top up, then choose: Mobile money, Credit card, or "I have a top-up code".
By mobile money, search for your country in the field, choose the operator (Orange Money, MTN MoMo or Moov Money depending on the country), then enter your number: the country code is already filled in. If the number belongs to another operator, the app corrects the operator by itself.
A country shown as "Coming soon" does not accept mobile money yet: top up by credit card or with a top-up code.
Enter the amount you want (shortcuts 1,000, 2,000, 5,000 or 10,000 F CFA), within the operator's limits, then tap Pay.
Depending on the operator, you receive an SMS with a link to tap (Orange Money in Côte d'Ivoire) or a secret code request on your phone. You have 15 minutes to confirm.
You can close the screen: a notification confirms the top-up, or explains the failure. When you reopen the top-up, the app resumes the pending request. If the confirmation comes after the 15 minutes, the balance is still credited.
This balance pays the Yadony service fee when the sender pays the traveler in cash, not a parcel.
