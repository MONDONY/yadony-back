Open the shipment in Activity › My parcels then tap "Cancel the request".
Before payment, nothing is charged and the traveler is notified.
Once the request is accepted, your payment is refunded automatically.
If the parcel has already been handed over, you are fully refunded and you get your parcel back: the traveler confirms the return by entering your return code.
If the traveler reschedules their trip, you can also cancel at no cost.
