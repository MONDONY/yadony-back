Open the shipment in Activity › My parcels then tap "Cancel the request".
Before payment, nothing is charged and the traveler is notified.
Once the request is accepted, your payment is refunded automatically. Depending on the parcel's date and the time left before departure, a cancellation fee may be kept from that refund (scale in the "Cancellation fee" article). When the preview is available, the app shows the exact amount before you confirm; if it says no fee applies, you are refunded in full. If no amount is shown because the preview is unavailable, the exact amount is kept according to the scale at the time of cancellation.
If the parcel has already been handed over but the trip has not left, your payment is refunded to you (subject to any cancellation fee, as above) and you get your parcel back within 3 days: the traveler confirms the return by entering your return code.
Once the parcel is on its way, or the departure has passed for a parcel already handed over, cancelling is no longer possible: if there is a problem, write to the team.
Boat parcel paid at drop-off: once the carrier has been paid when the parcel was dropped off, cancelling is refused and nothing is refunded automatically; contact support to open a dispute.
If the traveler reschedules their trip, you can also cancel at no cost.
If the Yadony team cancels your parcel (for example because the traveler cannot be reached), the parcel page shows "Cancelled by the Yadony team" and your payment is refunded. Tap "Contact support" on that page to follow up with the team.
