Pack the parcel carefully and describe its content precisely, with its approximate weight and at least one photo (required for the request): it protects both sides if you disagree.
If the traveler charges an item per unit (phone, laptop, suitcase…), choose that item rather than declaring it by the kilo: at handover, the traveler checks the parcel and can refuse one that doesn't match the declaration; you are then refunded.
Check that the photographed content matches the declared category.
Before handover, open your shipment in Activity › My parcels to show the drop-off QR: the traveler scans it at handover, and you can also save it, print it and stick it on the parcel.
Be present at handover and give the right recipient.
