Pack the parcel carefully and describe its content precisely, with its approximate weight and, if possible, photos that reassure the traveler.
Check that the photographed content matches the declared category.
Before handover, open your shipment in Activity › My parcels to show the drop-off QR: the traveler scans it at handover, and you can also save it, print it and stick it on the parcel.
Be present at handover and give the right recipient.
