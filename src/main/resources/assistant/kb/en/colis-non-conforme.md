As the traveler, check the parcel at handover. If it doesn't match what the sender declared (different content, for example a phone declared as 1 kg of clothes, heavier than declared or a prohibited item), open the parcel details, tap "Options" then "The parcel doesn't match".
Choose the reason, add details if needed and take a photo of the parcel: the photo is required, it serves as proof.
You can refuse it while the parcel is accepted, or already scanned but before the trip departs. If you already scanned it, give it back to the sender on the spot.
You get no penalty. The sender is notified with your reason and fully refunded if they paid by card or mobile money; for a cash payment, the Yadony commission you paid is given back to you. On the second refused parcel, the sender's account is suspended, unless the refusal follows an extra charge they declined or let expire.
The kilos of the refused parcel go back to your trip, which stays open for bookings if it hasn't departed yet: a full trip becomes available again. Refusing a parcel does not end your trip.
If the parcel is acceptable but worth more than planned (extra weight, item charged per unit), correct the price with an extra charge: "Options" › "Request an extra charge". The sender pays the difference or declines; if they decline, you carry the parcel at the agreed price or refuse it.
