Open Profile › ☰ › Settings › Information › Report a problem, describe the incident and attach screenshots.
On many screens, the "Report a problem" button automatically attaches a screenshot of the screen and you leave a message (type Bug, Feedback or Suggestion).
In Settings › Diagnostics, you see the app version and connection status, and you can copy your user ID, useful for support.
