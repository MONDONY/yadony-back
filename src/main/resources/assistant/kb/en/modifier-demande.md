A request can be edited as long as no offer has been accepted; after acceptance, contact support if important information must be corrected.
If travelers are already negotiating, editing it cancels all ongoing offers: they will have to propose a trip again.
The request's menu lets you unpublish it (it becomes a draft again), duplicate it for new dates or a new trip, or cancel it, which is irreversible.
When editing it, you can also remove the date: it becomes "Flexible date".
If the date has passed without a deal, republish it with new dates, or without a date. A request without a date closes on its own 60 days after it was posted.
