From a trip's page, you can write to the traveler before making a request. Your identity must be verified. You cannot write about your own trip, or about a trip that is no longer open.
Until you make a request, the conversation is limited to 10 messages from you, and a counter shows how many you have left. Only text messages are accepted: no photos at this stage.
As soon as you make your request, the limit is lifted and the messages already exchanged stay in the same conversation.
If the request does not go through (canceled, declined, expired), the 10-message limit comes back, without a reset: the messages you already sent still count.
A conversation with no request in progress (no request made, or a request that did not go through) is deleted after 30 days without activity.
