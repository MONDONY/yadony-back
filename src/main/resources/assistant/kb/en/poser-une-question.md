From a trip's page, you can write to the traveler before making a request. Your identity must be verified: otherwise the app first sends you to the verification. You cannot write about your own trip, or about a trip that is no longer open.
Until you make a request, the conversation is limited to 10 messages in total, and a counter shows how many are left. Only text messages are accepted: no photos at this stage.
As soon as you make your request, the limit is lifted and the messages already exchanged stay in the same conversation.
If the request does not go through (canceled, declined, expired), the 10-message limit comes back.
A conversation where no request was made is deleted after 30 days without activity.
