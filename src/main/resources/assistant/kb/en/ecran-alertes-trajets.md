You are on "My alerts": your alerts, grouped into "Watched trips" and "Watched parcels", with what's new for each. Tap "Create" to add one, "View" to open the trips or parcels found, and an alert's "Options" menu to "Edit", "Duplicate", "Pause" or "Delete" it. An expired alert can be restarted with "Extend".
