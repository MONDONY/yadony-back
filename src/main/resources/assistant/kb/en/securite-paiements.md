keywords: payment security, secure payment, card data, card stored, Stripe, PayPal, scam, pay outside the app, payment outside the platform, fraud

Online payments are processed by the payment providers shown in the app: the payment sheet displays "Payment secured by Stripe" and also offers PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay when they are available.
Yadony does not store your full card details.
By card, the money is held by Yadony and released only after the recipient confirms delivery. In cash, there is no escrow: Yadony does not hold the funds.
Never pay outside the flow provided in the app: a payment or agreement made outside the platform is not covered by Yadony.
