If your card is declined, try another card or contact your bank; if your bank's verification didn't go through, try again.
By mobile money, confirm the PIN request you receive on your phone and keep the screen open: confirmation is automatic.
If time runs out, the request is canceled and you make a new offer, or the price discussion goes back to "to pay" and you can resume the payment or change the payment method.
If the problem persists, contact support.
