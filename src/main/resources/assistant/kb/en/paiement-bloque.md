If your card is declined, try another card or contact your bank; if your bank's verification didn't go through, try again.
By mobile money, only the operators the traveler accepts are offered. An SMS with a link to tap (Orange) or a prompt asking for your secret code arrives on the phone that pays. You can close the screen: the app keeps following the payment and a notification confirms it.
After a refusal, the reason is shown (insufficient balance, payment not approved, limit reached, unusable number, operator unavailable, request expired): you can "Send a new request" or "Pay with a different number".
When the 30 minutes are up: if a request is still being approved with the operator, nothing is canceled until it is finished. The screen shows "Not approved yet"; if you approve, the payment goes through, and if it is refused, the payment is canceled. With no request in progress, the offer is canceled and you make a new offer, or the price discussion goes back to "to pay" and you can resume the payment or change the payment method.
If the problem persists, contact support.
