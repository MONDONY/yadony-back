If your card is declined, try another card or contact your bank; if your bank's verification didn't go through, try again.
By mobile money, only the operators the traveler accepts are offered. An SMS with a link to tap (Orange) or a window asking for your secret code arrives on the phone of the paying number. You can close the screen: the app keeps following the payment and a notification confirms it.
If time runs out while you are confirming, the screen shows "Not validated yet" and the payment is taken into account if you confirm the request.
After a refusal, the reason is shown (insufficient balance, payment not confirmed, limit reached, unusable number, operator unavailable, request expired): you can "Send a new request" or "Pay with another number".
If time runs out, the request is canceled and you make a new offer, or the price discussion goes back to "to pay" and you can resume the payment or change the payment method.
If the problem persists, contact support.
