In Messages, swipe a conversation then tap "Archive"; a long press lets you select several, and "Select finished" helps tidy the ones that are done.
Archived conversations are grouped under the "Archived" row, which opens the Archives: tap "Unarchive" to put one back in the list.
Deleting a conversation is permanent, for you and the other person.
