You are on Messages: all your conversations, grouped by date. Search with "Search conversations…" or filter by "All", "Unread", "Active" or "Done". Swipe a conversation to the right to "Mute" it, or to the left to "Archive" or "Delete" it; a long press selects several, with "Select finished". Your archived conversations are under the "Archived" row.
