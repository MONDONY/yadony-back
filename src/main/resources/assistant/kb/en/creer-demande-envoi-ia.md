In the parcel request form, tap "Create with AI", then describe your shipment out loud or in writing: cities, desired date and flexibility, weight, content, budget, payment method.
The AI asks one question at a time about what is missing, then fills in the form. Filled fields are marked "AI": check every step before publishing, nothing is published without you.
The AI adds no photo and no promo code: you add them in the form.
The microphone asks for your permission the first time; without it, you can type. The number of exchanges with the AI is limited each day.
