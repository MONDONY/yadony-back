You are in the price discussion for a parcel on a trip, between the sender and the traveler. The "Exchanges" section shows the proposals and the current round. When it is your turn, tap "Accept", "Counter-propose" or "Decline"; in the last round, only Accept and Decline remain. While you wait for a reply, "Cancel the negotiation" closes the discussion. Once the price is accepted, the sender taps "Pay", and in cash the traveler taps "Pay the service fee".
