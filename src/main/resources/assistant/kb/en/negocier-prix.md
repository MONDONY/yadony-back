On a negotiable trip, tap "Propose a price": the traveler can accept, refuse or reply with a counter-offer.
The discussion lasts 5 rounds at most, and the last round only allows accepting or refusing.
You follow all your discussions in Activity › Price discussions, where you can also send a reminder to the other person or cancel the negotiation.
Once the price is accepted, the sender pays to book their place; in cash, the traveler pays the Yadony service fee.
