If the recipient doesn't show up, the traveler first declares their arrival, waits the delay shown in the app and tries to reach the recipient or the sender from the app, then taps "Report the recipient as absent".
They then keep the parcel for the hold period shown.
The sender can set a new appointment or change the recipient, and can contest within 24 h.
With no delivery or contest by the end of the hold, the parcel becomes "unclaimed" and the traveler's payout is released.
