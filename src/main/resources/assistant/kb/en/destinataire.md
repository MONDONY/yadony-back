A new recipient is added when you choose the recipient of a shipment: a first and last name, which the traveler will see, and a number with the country code, for example +221, or tap "Choose from my contacts".
They are then kept in Activity › My recipients, where you can edit them, set one as default or add a Yadony recipient by phone number, email or Yadony identifier, then pick them in one tap when creating a request.
For a shipment you can choose a contact or a number, or "A Yadony user": enter their identifier and the app looks the account up ("This user does not exist" if it is not found; searches are limited to 30 per hour). You cannot designate yourself or the trip's traveler. The parcel goes ahead without a recipient number: it shows as "@identifier".
The wait for confirmation starts when the traveler accepts the parcel: the recipient gets a request and confirms or declines "this is for me". A trusted recipient (who already accepted your invitation) is confirmed automatically, with no request: their name and number, if they have not hidden it, appear as soon as the parcel is accepted. The pickup code stays valid during the wait. Their number only appears after they confirm, and only if they have not hidden it ("Hide my number" option). If they decline, you are notified and you designate another recipient: identifier, contact or number.
A parcel's recipient can be changed (identifier, name or number) until handover: the pickup code and tracking link are then renewed, and the former recipient no longer has access. Designating by identifier cannot be combined with a name and a number: one or the other.
Adding a Yadony recipient sends the person an invitation: they accept or decline. If they accept, they join your list, marked "In Yadony". If they decline, you get a notification and the invitation disappears from your "Sent invitations".
