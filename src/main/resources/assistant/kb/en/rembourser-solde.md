In My wallet, tap Refund, choose the currency then the top-up or top-ups to refund.
The amount goes back to the top-up method: the card used within 5 to 10 days depending on your bank, or the mobile money number that paid, usually within minutes; your balance is frozen while it is processed.
Payment provider fees may be withheld on a top-up that was never used, and bonuses cannot be refunded.
Follow your requests in "My refunds": In progress, Refunded or Failed.
