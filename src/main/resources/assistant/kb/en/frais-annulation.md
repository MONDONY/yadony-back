Depending on the parcel's date and the time left before departure, a cancellation fee may be kept from your refund. The app shows the exact amount and the sum refunded before you confirm; if no amount is shown, you are refunded in full.
When a fee applies, it depends on the time left before the trip's DEPARTURE (not the drop-off deadline): more than 5 days, 0%; from 5 to 3 days, 10%; from 3 days to 1 day, 20%; less than 1 day, departure day or after, 30%. It is capped at €30 per parcel (converted into the parcel's currency).
Who pays: only the sender at fault, meaning the one who cancels a parcel the traveler had accepted (or already received, before departure), or who does not hand over their parcel when the no-show is confirmed. The traveler is never charged when they cancel.
The sender is refunded the amount paid minus the fee. The traveler receives the fee, minus the payment provider's fees and Yadony's commission, on their Stripe account (card) or mobile money account (mobile money, after the sender's refund). They get a notification when this compensation is paid; the sender gets one too, with the amounts kept and refunded.
No fee in these cases: cash payment; parcel paid but not yet accepted by the traveler; trip rescheduled by the traveler and then the parcel withdrawn by the sender; traveler who cancels their trip or a parcel, or who does not show up (the sender is then fully refunded).
These fees only concern parcels created from an effective date: for an older parcel, no fee applies. To cancel, open the shipment in Activity › My parcels.
