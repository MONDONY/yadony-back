You are on the screen shown before you post a trip: it lists your commitments as a traveler (carry the parcel yourself, stick to the announced date and route, scan the QR code at drop-off and delivery, only accept allowed contents). If your identity is not verified yet, tap "Verify my identity". Otherwise, tick the box at the bottom of the page, then tap "Continue" to open the form. The "Activate card payments" banner, when shown, lets you set up Stripe.
