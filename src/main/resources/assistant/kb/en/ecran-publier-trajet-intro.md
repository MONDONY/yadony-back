You are on the screen shown before you post a trip. It lists your 15 commitments as a traveler, taken from the terms of use, in three blocks: before you leave, during transport, payment, disputes and taxes. No bank card needs to be saved to post. Scroll to the bottom: the "I have read…" box then unlocks, tick it and tap "Continue" to open the form. Your identity is checked when you post, in 2 minutes.
