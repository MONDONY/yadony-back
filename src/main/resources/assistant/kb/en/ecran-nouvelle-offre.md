You are making a request on a traveler's trip. Enter the weight or "Choose my items", the content, a description, at least one photo of the parcel (required) and the recipient, then tap "Continue". Then answer "How do you want to pay?" with one of the methods the traveler offers: by card, the amount is locked by Yadony and paid to the traveler once the recipient confirms delivery. If you are proposing your own price, fill in "YOUR PROPOSAL" then tap "Send my proposal".
