Your Yadony identifier is a short, unique name that lets a loved one add you as a recipient without knowing your phone number or email. It never shows your name or your phone number.
You can see it at the top of your profile, in a chip: tap it to copy it and share it.
To change it: Edit profile › Yadony identifier. The format is 3 to 20 characters: lowercase letters, digits, dot and underscore. You can change it once every 30 days, and the previous one stays reserved for 30 days, so nobody else can take it during that time.
To add a recipient with their identifier: My recipients › "Add a Yadony recipient", then the "Identifier" choice (next to Number and Email). The person receives an invitation and accepts or declines. If they accept, they join your list with their number.
For a specific parcel, when sending or changing the recipient, choose "A Yadony user" and enter their identifier: the parcel goes ahead without their number, and the person confirms or declines once the traveler accepts the parcel. Their number only appears after they confirm, and only if they have not hidden it.
The message "This user does not exist" means no account has that identifier: check the spelling with the person, as they may have changed it since. Identifier searches are limited to 30 per hour, sending invitations included.
