Follow your disputes in Profile › Tracking › My disputes. A dispute opens automatically when an absence is contested at a handover.
For another problem, for example a parcel that doesn't arrive, you don't open the dispute yourself: in My disputes, tap "A problem with a shipment?" (shown when the list is empty) to reach support, which opens the dispute. A dispute's detail page also has a "Contact support" button.
Give support the photos, messages and tracking information you have, and report the problem as soon as you notice it: the 15-day deadline after the expected date applies to a parcel that doesn't arrive.
During the review, the refund is frozen and the Yadony team decides within 72 business hours; the decision is shown in the dispute.
