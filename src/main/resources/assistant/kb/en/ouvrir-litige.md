Follow your disputes in Profile › Tracking › My disputes. A dispute opens automatically when an absence is contested at a handover.
For another problem, for example a parcel that doesn't arrive, contact support with the photos, messages and tracking information you have (My disputes offers "A problem with a shipment?" when it is empty).
During the review, the refund is frozen and the Yadony team decides within 72 business hours; the decision is shown in the dispute.
Report the problem as soon as you notice it: the applicable deadlines are reminded in the reporting flow.
