You are on the Tracking tab, which has two modes. "Validate a step" is for the traveler: pick your trip and scan a parcel's QR code, and the next step (collection, optional transit, arrival) is validated for you; if the QR code is unreadable, tap "QR unreadable? Enter the number". "Track a parcel" validates nothing: scan a QR code or enter the tracking number to see where the parcel is, and find your parcels under "Sending" and "Receiving".
