Top to bottom: the ticket, a status card ("Trip arrived: your parcels are waiting for their recipients" after arrival; "Contact support" link if the trip is full or removed), "To do" (Requests, I'm leaving, "Add card to this trip" only with no accepted parcel), Requests (gone once the trip is over), Parcels (first 2 parcels, total, "See the N other parcels", "See list"), Estimated earnings, "Your trip". The sticky button offers the action of the moment. ⋮ menu: Edit, Reschedule, Unpublish, Share the poster, Pickup instructions, recurring, template, Cancel, Delete.
