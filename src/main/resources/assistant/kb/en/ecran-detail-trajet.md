Ticket and kilo and price pills at the top. A status card only for a draft, full, arrived, canceled or removed trip. "To do": the "Request 1 of N" card (Decline, Accept, "See all N requests" link), then "I'm leaving" and "Add card to this trip". "Trip activity": Requests, Parcels ("Open parcel list"), Earnings, each opens a window. "Your trip": Route and places (legs), Payments, Conditions, Poster, Arrival. The sticky button follows the state. ⋮ menu: Edit, Reschedule, Unpublish, Share the poster, Pickup instructions, recurring, template, Cancel, Delete.
