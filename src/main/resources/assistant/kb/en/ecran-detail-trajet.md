Top to bottom: the ticket with its kilos and price pills, a status card, "To do" (Requests to review, I'm leaving, card), the Requests, Parcels (first 2 parcels, total, "See the N other parcels", "See list") and Estimated earnings cards, then "Your trip" (Route and places, Payments, Conditions, Poster, Arrival). The sticky bottom button offers the action of the moment. The ⋮ menu: Edit, Reschedule, Unpublish, Share the poster, Pickup instructions, Make this trip recurring, Save as template, Cancel the trip, Delete, depending on the trip's state.
