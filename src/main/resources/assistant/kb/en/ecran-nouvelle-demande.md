You are filling in a shipping request in three steps: "The trip" (where from, where to, date and flexibility), "The parcel" (approximate weight, content, description, photos) then "The budget". For the price, choose "I'm open to offers" or "I set my price", then the accepted payment methods. "Preview" lets you check everything before "Post my request" or "Save as draft". Once posted, travelers on this trip are notified.
