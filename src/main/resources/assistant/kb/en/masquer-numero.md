Privacy is set in Profile › ☰ › Settings › Security & data › Privacy.
"Hide my number" keeps your number from being shared, even after an accepted offer: your exchanges then go through Yadony messaging.
"Verified profiles only" limits offers to users who have verified their identity.
You can also choose whether to show your country of residence and your last connection on your public profile.
