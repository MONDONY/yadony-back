The law (European DAC7 directive, articles 1649 ter A to E of the French tax code) requires platforms like Yadony to report each year to the French tax authority the amounts received by some travelers.
Who is concerned: only a traveler who lives in France or in another European Union country and who has already been paid at least once through Yadony. If you are not concerned, nothing is asked of you.
What is reported, per quarter: the amount received after Yadony fees, the number of parcels and the Yadony fees, with your name, date of birth, address and tax number.
What Yadony may ask you: only your tax identification number (TIN), the country that issued it and, if you have one, your VAT number. Your name, date of birth and address are never asked again.
If this feature is available in your version of the app, the "Tax information" screen is in your profile. Your number stays masked and encrypted there.
Nothing is blocked: publishing a trip, accepting a parcel, getting paid or withdrawing your money never depends on this information. You can answer "Later".
You receive at most two reminders, and they follow your notification settings.
After the annual declaration, the team may send you a summary of the amounts reported.
For a question about your tax situation, contact the tax authority of your country or an adviser. For a question about the app, write to support in the Messages tab.
