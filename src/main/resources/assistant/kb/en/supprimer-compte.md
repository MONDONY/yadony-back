In the Profile tab, open the ☰ menu then "Delete my account".
You choose between a reversible 30-day pause, during which you can come back, and an immediate, permanent deletion of your personal data.
On permanent deletion, your identity verification (document and selfie) is also erased at our verification provider, and your bank payout account is closed. If money or a payout is still on it, the Yadony team settles it before closing it.
An ongoing transaction can block the deletion: a shipment being delivered with funds held in escrow must be delivered first.
If you have a Yadony balance, it is refunded after the deletion, and you can also request the refund right now.
