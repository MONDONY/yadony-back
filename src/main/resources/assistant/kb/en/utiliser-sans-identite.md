title: Use Yadony without verifying my identity
keywords: without verification, without identity, browse, explore, use without account, identity required, why
---
You can browse listings without verifying your identity.
Some actions, notably sending a parcel, carrying parcels or receiving payments, may require verification.
When an action needs it, the app shows "Verification required" with a button to start it.
With no account at all, access is limited to search.
