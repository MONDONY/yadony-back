You are in Settings. Here you set the "Theme" and "Language", then "Security" (biometrics, PIN, sessions), "Privacy" and "My data". The Personalization section groups "You use Yadony to…" (which also sets whether the home screen opens on "I'm sending a parcel" or "I'm traveling"), "Notifications", "Preferences" (kg/lbs, currency, collection radius) and "Accessibility". At the bottom you will find the "Terms of Use", the "Privacy Policy", "Report a problem" and "Diagnostics".
