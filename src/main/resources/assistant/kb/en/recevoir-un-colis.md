If you have Yadony, parcels meant for you appear in the Tracking tab, in "Track a parcel" mode, under Receiving.
Open one and tap "Yes, it's for me" to follow it and get your pickup code, or "It's not for me" to remove it from your list.
No app yet? Install Yadony and sign up with the number the sender entered (the one shown on the tracking page): the parcel shows up on its own under Receiving and a notification lets you know.
Your code is secret: give it to the traveler only at handover.
A sender can also add you to their recipients: accept or decline their request in Profile › Tracking › Authorized senders.
