In cash, the sender hands the sum to the traveler by hand at parcel handover.
There is no escrow: Yadony does not hold the funds and cannot refund automatically in case of a dispute.
The traveler pays the Yadony commission on acceptance, from their wallet balance first (that is what the balance is for); otherwise they top up or save a valid card in Profile › Money › Cash service fee card.
If the card has expired, they must replace it to re-enable cash payment.
