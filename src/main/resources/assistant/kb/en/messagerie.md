Your conversations appear in the Messages tab after an offer is accepted; the "Active" filter (the default) shows everything not finished and any conversation with an unread message, "Done" shows delivered or canceled exchanges already read.
For your safety, keep exchanges and payment on Yadony: sharing contact details, external links and bank details is blocked.
Sending photos is only possible once the request is accepted and paid.
You can reply to a message, copy it or report it, and edit or delete your own within 15 minutes.
