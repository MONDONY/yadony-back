Verification starts from Profile › My account › ID documents, or as soon as an action requires it (sending a parcel, publishing a trip, receiving payments).
Accepted documents: national ID card, driver's license, passport, residence permit or residence card. The document must be valid, legible and unexpired. You also need a selfie: the app needs the camera.
If everything goes well, validation is immediate at the end of the process; otherwise the app invites you to start again.
Verification may be required by our payment partners and also helps fight fraud.
Once validated, an "Identity verified" badge appears on your profile.
If you start without finishing, or if verification fails for a reason you can fix (photo, expired document), Yadony sends you up to 3 reminders, by notification and by e-mail, within 7 days. The link at the bottom of the e-mail turns them off for good.
