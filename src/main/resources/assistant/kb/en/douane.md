The traveler declares what they are carrying.
In the app, every parcel comes with a description and the photos taken at handover, which the traveler can show during a check.
Any duties and taxes remain payable by the sender.
That is also the reason for the list of forbidden items: a parcel held up at customs holds up the person carrying it. See "Which items are forbidden?".
