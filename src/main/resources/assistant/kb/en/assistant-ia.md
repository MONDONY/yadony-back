The assistant answers your questions about the app, tells you the way and offers a button to open the right screen.
It is in the draggable bubble shown on most screens, and in the header of the Messages tab.
It is an AI that answers from Yadony's help notes: it can be wrong.
It can also talk about your parcels: it offers them, or finds the one whose DON-… tracking number you type, and answers according to its state (step, cancellation, refund, pickup code, return). It only sees parcels you send, carry or receive, and never gives a code: it opens the screen that shows it.
For another problem specific to your account, or if the answer isn't enough, it offers to write to the team, which creates a support ticket.
Before your first question, and before your first “Create with AI”, the app asks for your consent: your questions, or the text you dictate, go to an AI provider outside Yadony (currently Anthropic, Claude model), without your name, phone number, email or parcel tracking numbers. If you decline, nothing is sent and the assistant closes; you will be asked again next time you open it, and after you sign out.
