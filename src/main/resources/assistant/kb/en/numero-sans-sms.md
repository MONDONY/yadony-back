In some countries, the SMS code never arrives. If your number is in one of them, the app offers "Save without verifying": no SMS is sent and the number is saved as you entered it (Profile › ☰ › Edit profile › Contact details › Phone).
It then shows the "Not verified" badge. You can correct it as many times as needed as long as it stays in one of those countries.
A Yadony team member can check your number by hand: it then shows "Verified by Yadony", not to be confused with "Verified", which means a number confirmed by SMS code. If you change the number afterwards, the badge disappears and the number goes back to "Not verified".
A declared number is only used to let others reach you (sender, traveler or recipient can call you) and to let Yadony find your account. It is never used to sign in, nor for payments, payouts or refunds: for mobile money, you enter the number to use at the time of payment.
A declared number does not automatically attach parcels or invitations sent to that number to your account: only a number verified by SMS or by Yadony does.
The "Hide my number" setting also applies to this number.
If your country later accepts SMS, you can verify the number by code: it replaces the declared one. A number already verified by SMS cannot be replaced this way.
