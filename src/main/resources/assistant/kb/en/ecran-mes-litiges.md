You are on "My disputes", the list of your disputes: tap one to see its details and the decision. A dispute opens automatically if you contest a traveler's no-show at drop-off. For any other problem with a shipment, tap "A problem with a shipment?" (shown when the list is empty) to reach support. During the review, the refund is on hold and the answer comes within 72h.
