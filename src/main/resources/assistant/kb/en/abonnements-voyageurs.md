From a traveler's profile, tap "Follow": you are notified of their new trips.
To avoid bursts, your phone only rings once until you open that notification or their profile: trips published in the meantime arrive in your notifications, without a sound.
Each traveler's push alerts can be turned on or off; without them, their trips only arrive in your notifications.
Find all the travelers you follow in Profile › Tracking › Following, where you can also unfollow.
