You are on Referral. Copy your invitation link or code, then tap "Share" or WhatsApp to send it to your friends and family. The reward unlocks when a friend's first parcel is delivered: then tap "Choose my reward" (credit on your balance or a discount code). Your referrals and counters ("Signed up", "First shipment done", "Earned") are tracked here. If someone invited you, tap "Have a referral code? Enter it".
