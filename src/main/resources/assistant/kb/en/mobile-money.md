To pay, choose your country, the operator (only those the traveler accepts are offered) and the paying number, yours or a relative's, then confirm the request you receive (SMS with a link, or secret code): after the traveler accepts, you have 30 minutes. Yadony holds the money until delivery.
To get paid, open Profile › Money › Mobile money payout then "Add an account": choose the country, the operator and the number (it can differ from your Yadony number), then check it in "Is this your number?". You have one account per operator and per country, each with its own number, and you can have accounts in several countries. An account can be neither removed nor given a new number while a parcel paid on it is waiting for its payout.
Mobile money is offered for trips in XOF or XAF. The sender can only pay with an operator for which you have an account in the trip's currency (they see the accepted operators on the trip page), and you are paid at delivery on the account of the same operator and country as their payment.
